Manufacturing Plant Internal Audit — Fremont, California

Context: Motherson Internal Audit Internship Location: Fremont, CA Year: 2025

Overview

During my 2025 Internal Audit internship with Motherson, I participated in an on-site audit of a manufacturing facility in Fremont, California. The audit covered a broad range of business processes, including financial processes, HR/payroll, inventory, IT, and other operational areas. Some areas had been reviewed in advance before the team arrived on-site.

My Role

My responsibilities included tracking which requested records and supporting documents had been received and which were still outstanding. I participated in team meetings with employees and managers as part of the audit process. I also observed physical processes on the plant floor, particularly inventory-related procedures.

Testing and Analysis

I personally conducted testing using submitted documentation. This included verifying information such as punchcard hours and hourly pay for accuracy.

Tools Used

  • Microsoft Excel: Used for documentation verification and testing analysis.
  • CAMS (Compliance Audit Management System): Used for tracking audit documentation and metrics.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Documentation tracking
  • Attention to detail
  • Business-process analysis
  • Audit testing
  • Professional communication
  • Teamwork and collaboration
  • Observing operational controls

Key Takeaway

This experience gave me practical exposure to how an internal audit team evaluates business processes across multiple functional areas and how documentation, testing, and on-site observation work together in an audit engagement.
